Sıkça Sorulan Sorular

Promosyon kodu nasıl kullanılır?

1. Kodu kopyalayın.
2. Ortağın web sitesine gidin.
3. Kodu sepete girin.

Kod neden çalışmıyor?

Son kullanma tarihini veya koşulları kontrol edin. Sorun devam ederse, support@serpmonn.ru adresine yazın.

Where do the promo codes come from?

Offers from Serpmonn partners (ad networks and direct placements). The advertiser is listed on each card.

Which countries are supported?

Mostly Russia; some offers also cover Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, and Georgia. Use the country filter above the catalog.

Do I need an account?

No. You can copy a code without signing in. Email subscription is optional for new deals.

How do I share a promo code?

Tap Share on the card to get a link to that code.

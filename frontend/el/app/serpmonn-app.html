FAQ

How is the APK different from the store build?

It’s the same Serpmonn app. Play and RuStore deliver updates automatically; the site APK helps when a store isn’t available — Android may show a warning, that’s expected.

Do I need an account?

Not for news and some tools. An account is required for messages, the findings feed, and profile sync.

Are there ads?

No ads in the app. It’s free to use.

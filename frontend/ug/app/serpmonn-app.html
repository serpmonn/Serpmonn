FAQ

Where should I install from?

Google Play or RuStore is easiest — updates arrive automatically. The site APK works when a store is unavailable; the system may show a warning, and that is expected.

How is the APK different from the store build?

It’s the same Serpmonn app. Play and RuStore deliver updates automatically; the site APK helps when a store isn’t available — Android may show a warning, that’s expected.

Do I need an account?

Not for news and some tools. An account is required for messages, the findings feed, and profile sync.

Is the app free?

Yes — free to download and use, with no ads. AI search has a daily limit; Pro unlocks more.

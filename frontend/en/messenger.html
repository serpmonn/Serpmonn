How it works

On-device encryption. Direct connection. Local history.

End-to-end

Messages are encrypted on the device and decrypted only on the recipient’s side. Servers and Serpmonn never see the content.

Direct P2P

Devices connect directly. If the network blocks that, a fallback encrypted route kicks in — E2E stays intact.

Offline and groups

Bluetooth and local Wi‑Fi without the internet. Personal and group chats, QR contacts, history on the device.
